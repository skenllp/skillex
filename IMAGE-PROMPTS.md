# SKILLEX image prompts

Set each finished file's path in `lib/images.ts` (files go in `public/images/`).
Export as .webp, under about 250 KB each. Until a path is set, nothing is requested.

Style line to append to every prompt:
"Natural daylight, clean modern interior, soft realistic colours with subtle navy and lime-green accents, candid documentary style, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no watermark, no distorted hands or faces."

Prefer real photos of your own campus and students. Use AI images only as a stand-in.

1. campus-exterior.webp (4:3 or 16:8) - campusExterior
   Front of a modern two-storey commercial building in a small Kerala town, glass entrance, clear morning light, a few students walking in.
2. classroom.webp (16:10) - classroom
   Bright training hall with rows of desks and laptops, a presenter's screen at the front, six to eight adult learners listening, windows on one side.
3. practical-training.webp (16:10) - practicalTraining
   Two or three learners working at laptops on accounting spreadsheets and a marketing dashboard, a trainer leaning in to help.
4. student-environment.webp (16:10) - studentEnvironment
   Small group of young adults chatting and reviewing notes in a bright lounge area with sofas and plants.
5. about-team.webp (4:3) - aboutTeam
   Four or five trainers and staff standing together in a bright office, smiling, casual business attire.
6. mentor-guidance.webp (4:3) - mentorGuidance
   A mentor sitting beside a student at a desk, pointing at a laptop screen, warm natural light.
7. course-accounting.webp (21:9) - courseAccounting
   Overhead desk with a laptop showing a spreadsheet, a calculator, a notebook and a pen.
8. course-sales-hr.webp (21:9) - courseSalesHr
   Two professionals in a friendly interview or team meeting at a table, notes and a tablet in front of them.
9. course-digital-marketing.webp (21:9) - courseDigitalMarketing
   Laptop and phone on a desk showing analytics charts and social layouts, coffee cup, soft daylight.

Negative prompt: text, captions, logos, brand names, extra fingers, warped faces, stock-photo handshake, heavy filters.
